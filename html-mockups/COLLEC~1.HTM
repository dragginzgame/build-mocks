Vendor rail & collection links.

Response to the dev note on pic 1. The public collection page is missing the Active vendors preview rail that public-facing-collection.md already specifies — today the only route to a launchpad vendor is via Launchpad itself. Part A adds that rail, deep-linking to /launchpad?collection=<collectionId>. Part B adds the reverse link: the collection name becomes a link back to the public collection page from marketplace browse, the listed-token page and My Tokens. Part C states the aggregate rule the devs asked us to make official — launchpad claims never feed floor, 24h volume, sales or owners; those are secondary-market metrics only, and claim counts live on the vendor rail instead.

Part C

Aggregates — claims are not trades.

Making the devs' assumption official, and giving it a home in the UI so nobody has to ask again.

C1What counts whereFloor, 24h volume, sales, owners and listed are secondary-market figures computed from marketplace trades only. Launchpad claims are primary distribution: they change supply and ownership, so they move owners and minted, but they never contribute a price to floor / volume / average price / sales count. Claim throughput is surfaced on the vendor rail as minted-vs-supply, labelled separately.
Secondary market — trades only
FloorVolume · 24h / 7d / allSales countAverage priceListed

Source: completed marketplace sales and active listings. A claim never appears in this set, at any price, including priced claims.

Primary distribution — claims
Claimed / supplyVendors liveLatest mint

Source: vendor claim events. Shown on the vendor rail and on the vendor page — never merged into the market metrics row.

Shared
OwnersTotal supply

Ownership and supply change on both paths, so these two legitimately move on a claim. They carry no price, so there's no volume ambiguity.

In the UI this is carried by the metric sublines — Secondary, Trades only, On marketplace — plus the one line under the vendor rail heading: "Claims are primary distribution and don't affect the market metrics above." That's the whole disclosure; no banner, because this is product information, not a user alert.
