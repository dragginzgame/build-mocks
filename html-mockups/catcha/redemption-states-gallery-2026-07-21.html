Physical redemption.

How an owner exchanges a vaulted token for its physical item — RWA collections only (every redeemable token is a 1-of-1 backed by an item in vault custody; no other archetype ever shows a Redeem action). Split out of the my-tokens gallery 2026-07-21: redemption is a later-phase feature, and the V1 wallet gallery no longer shows it. Ruled 2026-07-21: the holding locks the moment Redeem is pressed — no sale, transfer, burn or listing while a redemption is open; the owner pays shipping (directly to the vault) or cancels; an unpaid request auto-cancels after 30 days; payment makes it a binding fulfilment; the token becomes Redeemed (terminal) at tracking entry. All consumer copy — never backend state names.

Source: Design Documentation/Phase2/vault-provider-system-design.md §7 · graded-slabs-design.md §5. Related: My Tokens (canonical detail anatomy) · Vault management (the vault's side) · RWA archetype.
